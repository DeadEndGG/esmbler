## Start or resume here

1. Read `docs/spec/execution-brief.md` (how to build) and `docs/spec/master-spec.md` (what to build).
2. Read `docs/progress.md` to find where work stands.
3. Continue from there. Do not stop to ask for approval or whether to continue; follow the autonomy rules in section 1 of the execution brief.

- Tenant and environment isolation is never deferred or stubbed.
- Never edit `docs/spec/master-spec.md` or `docs/spec/execution-brief.md`; record deviations as ADRs or in `docs/decisions/assumptions.md`.
- Update `docs/progress.md` after every milestone.
