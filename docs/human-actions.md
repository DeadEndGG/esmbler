| Provide a support address and route its mail to Esmbler (inbound) | Email intake ([ADR 0023](adr/0023-email-intake.md)): messages to a workspace address open and continue tickets | Before the Gate 2 pilot | Mailboxes work end to end through the webhook with test messages (integration tests, Playwright, `curl` with a raw `.eml`); nothing receives real mail. For both, enter the **authserv-id** of the inbound mail server that adds `Authentication-Results` (for example `mx.example.com`; it is the first word of that header) as `authservId`, so senders are verified; `senderVerification = none` trusts any From address, needs permission administration and is only for tests | open |
