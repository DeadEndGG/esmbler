# Esmbler.Sdk

The public SDK: everything first-party and third-party modules may depend on (dogfooding rule, REQ-MOD-001).

- `Specs/`: spec model (entities, fields, rules, workflows with active-instance policies, views/layout nodes, roles, permission sets, migrations, catalog items and request bindings in `Specs/Requests.cs`, approval policies and the approval binding in `Specs/Approvals.cs`, event types and emits in `Specs/Events.cs` (ADR 0017), business calendars and SLA policies in `Specs/Sla.cs` (ADR 0018), metrics in `Specs/Metrics.cs` (SLA states `breached` and `at_risk` in `MetricSlaStates`; alert metrics through `MetricAlertSpec` and `MetricAlertFields`, G2-M9c) and dashboard views (`ViewKinds.Dashboard`) (ADR 0019), notification subscriptions, profiles, routes and recipients in `Specs/Notifications.cs` and `NotificationTemplateSpec` in `Specs/Logic.cs` (variants, external fields; `Event` and `Entity` nullable since G2-M18, a deliberate source-breaking nullability change for C# module authors, ASM-086) (ADR 0021), alert rules (`AlertRuleSpec`, owners, escalation steps, `AlertStatuses`) in `Specs/Alerts.cs` with `SubscriptionTargets.Alert` and the recipient kind `alert_owner` (ADR 0022), mail intakes (`MailIntakeSpec`), `MailConventions.ReplyMarker` (the line above which reply text is kept; put it at the top of emails people may answer) and `MailReasons` in `Specs/Mail.cs` (ADR 0023; no overlay ops yet), platform actions in `Specs/Experience.cs` (`PlatformActions`, including `notifications.manage`, `alerts.respond`, `alerts.manage`, `audit.export` and `audit.manage`); field type `principals` for platform-maintained user and group lists). Serialized with `SpecJson` (camelCase, snake_case enums).
- `Overlay/`: typed overlay operations (Command pattern, 54 ops including `view.add|remove` (tenant dashboards), `alert_rule.add|update|remove`, `subscription.*`, `notification_template.*`, `notification_profile.*`, `notification_route.*`, `approval_policy.add|update|remove`, `event_type.add|remove`, `business_calendar.add|update|remove`, `sla_policy.add|update|remove` and `metric.add|update|remove`) and namespace rules (`tenant__` prefix). Ops that change who approves (an item's `approvalPolicy`, a policy's stages, policy removal), who may run a transition, who sees a figure (`metric.update` with `roles`), whether a route is mandatory, and who outside the workspace is told and what they see (external recipients, `ExternalFields`) report `AffectsPermissions`; `ConfigCompiler.PermissionDiff` also flags them at publish. `alert_rule.*` ops are not permission ops; silencing a rule behind a mandatory alert route is caught by `PermissionDiff` (ADR 0022).
- `Registry/`: element registry model and the catalog (`Registry/Elements/*.json`, schema `Schemas/element.schema.json`), including `display.kpi_tile`, `display.bar_chart`, `display.metric_table`, `spec_object.metric`, `collection.data_table` 1.1.0 (`groupBy`, `groupOptions`) and `collection.saved_views` 1.1.0 (items may reference a metric).
- `Modules/`: `IModuleDefinition`, release loading, `SemVer`, and starters (`Modules/Starters.cs`: `StarterSpec`, `StarterGroupSpec`; `IModuleDefinition.Starters` defaults to empty) for guided workspace creation (ADR 0020). A starter adds no configuration: it names the teams to create (group keys must match the module's assignment rules) and asks a `Question` only when a team has no sensible default name. `Events/`: event envelope (v2, read alongside v1) and platform event type IDs (including `platform.alert.*` and `EventTypes.IsAlert`). `Notifications/`: `INotificationChannel` (one send, `ChannelResult` sent, transient, permanent or unknown, `SupportsIdempotency`), `ChannelMessage` (with email threading properties `MessageId`, `References`, `ReplyTo` and `AutoSubmitted`, default true, ADR 0023) and `ISecretAccessor` (ADR 0021); the contract is public, but adapters are platform-only for now (registered by `Esmbler.Hosting`, not through `IModuleDefinition`).
- Invariants: no references to internal projects; additive, backward-compatible changes only (a deliberate exception needs an ADR or assumption entry, as ASM-086 for nullable template `Event` and `Entity`); every element/spec change updates schemas and generated docs.
- Tests: `dotnet test tests/Esmbler.UnitTests`. Docs: `docs/reference/element-registry-schema.md`, `docs/adr/0003-configuration-artifact-and-overlay-format.md`.
